# Media and Rights Notes

This research library intentionally does not redistribute manufacturer/editorial images or downloaded videos.

## Why
Many OEM press sites permit editorial use but require separate approval for advertising, commercial application, or other reuse. Third-party editorial photography is also copyrighted.

## Safe WTRL workflow
1. Use linked images/videos for private reference and engineering study.
2. Record factual observations and dimensions, not copied artistic composition.
3. Create original WTRL vehicle models, textures, renders, diagrams and screenshots.
4. Maintain distinctive fictional branding, lighting signatures, body details, badges and trade dress.
5. Obtain explicit licenses before shipping any third-party image, clip or logo.

## Media capture schema
For each useful reference:
- source ID
- URL
- exact vehicle/configuration
- timestamp/frame if video
- visible subsystem
- observation
- factual extraction
- WTRL transformation
- rights status
