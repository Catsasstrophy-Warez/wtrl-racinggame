# Specification Normalization Rules

Before using a real-world figure as WTRL calibration truth:

1. Resolve exact model year, trim, market and transmission.
2. Record rating system: SAE gross, SAE net, DIN, PS, kW, manufacturer claim or independent test.
3. Keep manufacturer curb weight separate from magazine test weight.
4. Record test conditions for acceleration, braking and skidpad figures.
5. Never merge race-car and road-car specifications under one model name.
6. Treat homologation specials, continuation cars, replicas and film cars as separate records.
7. Use OEM technical sheets first; period instrumented tests second; specialist archives third.
8. For fictional WTRL derivatives, preserve the engineering principle but author original final values.

## Simulation-use levels

- `FACT`: exact supported specification.
- `ENVELOPE`: range from several real references.
- `AUTHORED`: WTRL-original value inspired by research.
- `UNVERIFIED`: research lead only.
