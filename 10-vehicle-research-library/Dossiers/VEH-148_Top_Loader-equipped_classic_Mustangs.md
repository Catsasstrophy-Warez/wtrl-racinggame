## Cross-reference note (technical-category pass, not a standalone vehicle)
This dossier is a real Ford transmission-family reference (the Ford Toploader 4-speed manual, so named for its top-loaded gear cluster), not a distinct vehicle.

**Real transmission specs (web-verified this pass):**
- Introduced 1963; used in most Ford/Mercury performance applications 1964-1973, including Mustangs.
- Two common ratio sets, distinguished by input/output shaft size:
  - **Close-ratio (large-shaft, big-block-oriented):** 1st 2.32 / 2nd 1.69 / 3rd 1.29 / 4th 1.00
  - **Wide-ratio (small-shaft, small-block-oriented):** 1st 2.78 / 2nd 1.93 / 3rd 1.36 / 4th 1.00
- Both sets are a straight 1:1 (no overdrive) top gear.

**Cross-reference against already-researched dossiers in this library:**
- **VEH-023 (1969 Boss 429)** already lists "4-speed manual (Toploader)" as its verified transmission — this is the one confirmed hard match in the library.
- **VEH-016 (1965 GT350)** lists a 4-speed manual without naming Toploader specifically; VEH-019 (1967 GT500) lists "4-speed manual or C6 auto"; VEH-020 (1968 GT390 Bullitt) lists "4-speed manual (film car)." Toploaders were the standard Ford 4-speed of this era, so these are plausible Toploader applications, but those dossiers do not themselves specify the transmission by name — flagged honestly rather than asserted as confirmed.
