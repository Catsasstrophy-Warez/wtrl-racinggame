## Specification status
This is the real 1974 H.B. Halicki original film's actual "Eleanor" car — a 1971 Ford Mustang SportsRoof fitted with a 1973 Mustang front grille/facelift and Mach 1-style blackout side treatment, distinct from both the fictional badge-name and the 2000 remake's GT500-based build (VEH-044).

## Verified specification snapshot (real base car for the 1974 film)
- Base vehicle: 1971 Ford Mustang SportsRoof, refaced with a 1973 Mustang grille (not a real GT500 or Shelby-based car)
- Engine: stock Ford 351 cubic inch (5.8L) Windsor V8 with four-barrel carburetor (as fitted to the film car; factory 351W 4V output in this era was commonly rated in the mid-200s hp gross/net range across contemporary Mustang literature, but no single independently-verified dyno/factory figure for this specific film car was found in this pass)
- Transmission: Ford C6 "Cruisamatic" 3-speed automatic
- Wheels/tires: styled steel wheels, Goodyear Rally GT tires
- Electrical: reported 24-volt electrical system fitted for the stunt car's heavy-duty use
- Production note: a dedicated "hero" car was used for static/exterior shots and a separate "stunt" car (reportedly requiring roughly 250 hours of prep labor) was used for driving/stunt scenes
- Curb weight/wheelbase: not captured with a single citable figure in this pass; use 1971 Mustang SportsRoof production data as an envelope
- Evidence: SRC-231, SRC-232, SRC-233
