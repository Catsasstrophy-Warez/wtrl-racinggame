## Verified specification snapshot
Snapshot is for the 991.2-generation 911 GT2 RS (2018).
- Engine: 3.8L twin-turbocharged flat-six (water-cooled, Vario-Cam Plus)
- Power: 700 PS / 700 hp (515 kW)
- Torque: 750 Nm (553 lb-ft)
- Transmission: 7-speed PDK dual-clutch
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: 2,450 mm (standard 991-platform figure; not separately confirmed for GT2 RS)
- Curb weight: 1,470 kg (with full fuel tank, DIN)
- Evidence: SRC-143
