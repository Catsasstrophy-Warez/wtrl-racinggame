## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 4.6L SOHC 2-valve V8 (Modular family)
- Power: 250 hp @ 5,000 rpm (later FFV/flex-fuel-capable tune; earlier years as low as 224 hp depending on model year/exhaust)
- Torque: 297 lb-ft @ 4,000 rpm (later years); as low as 275 lb-ft on earlier tunes
- Transmission: 4R70W heavy-duty 4-speed automatic overdrive
- Drive layout: RWD, 8.8-in rear axle with Trac-Loc limited-slip, 3.27 or 3.55 police-spec final drive
- Wheelbase: not captured in this pass
- Curb weight: not captured in this pass
- Evidence: SRC-034; SRC-102
