## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: base 144 ci / 2.4L I6 (85 bhp); optional 170 ci / 2.8L I6 (101 bhp); optional 260 ci / 4.3L V8 (164 bhp)
- Power: 164 bhp @ 4400 rpm (260 V8 top option)
- Torque: not sourced in this pass
- Transmission: 3-speed manual standard; Ford-O-Matic automatic optional
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: 109.5 in
- Curb weight: not sourced in this pass
- Evidence: SRC-110

Note: the 1963 Falcon is the direct chassis/platform ancestor of the 1965 Mustang (VEH-015) and is retained here as a packaging/architecture reference rather than a Mustang variant.
