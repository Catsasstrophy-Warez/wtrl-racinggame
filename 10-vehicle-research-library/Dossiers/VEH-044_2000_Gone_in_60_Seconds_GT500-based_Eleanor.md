## Specification status
This is the real 2000 remake's actual "Eleanor" hero/stunt cars, built by Cinema Vehicle Services with body design by Steve Stanford and execution credited to Chip Foose, on a real 1967 Ford Mustang fastback base dressed with a custom Shelby GT500-style kit — distinct from both the real 1967 factory GT500 and the fictional badge concept.

## Verified specification snapshot (real production cars for the 2000 film)
- Base vehicle: 1967 Ford Mustang fastback, custom-bodied with a Shelby GT500-style kit (Dupont Pepper Grey paint with black racing stripes)
- Engine: 351 cubic inch (5.8L) Windsor-based V8 crate engine, reported at approximately 400 hp (builder/press-reported figure, not an independent dyno certification)
- Transmission: 4-speed manual
- Rear axle: Ford 9-inch rear end
- Suspension/brakes: custom/lowered coilover suspension, Wilwood disc brakes
- Wheels/tires: 17-inch wheels with Goodyear Eagle F1 tires
- Special effects: a "faux" nitrous-oxide activation effect was fitted for on-screen use (not a functional nitrous system)
- Production count: reportedly eleven Eleanor cars were built for the film, of which only three were fully functional "driver" cars and two of those were destroyed during filming
- Builder: Cinema Vehicle Services, design by Steve Stanford, build execution associated with Chip Foose
- Evidence: SRC-230, SRC-231
