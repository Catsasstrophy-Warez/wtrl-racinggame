## Verified specification snapshot
The GT500E is a real, distinct commercial continuation/restomod product: a licensed 1967 Mustang-based build (donor cars restored with new drivetrain/interior/suspension) produced by Unique Performance under a Carroll Shelby license beginning around 2002-2004, related to but not identical to the "Eleanor" screen car from *Gone in 60 Seconds* (2000).
- Base/standard build: Shelby-tuned V8, 325 hp
- High-performance/all-out build option: reported ~725 hp for one specially-built example (non-standard, customer-specific configuration, single build reported)
- Transmission: 6-speed manual (standard build)
- Drive layout: RWD
- Options noted: nitrous oxide system, roll bar, 17-in wheels, side exhaust, Baer brakes
- Pricing (era-specific, not a spec but relevant to positioning): standard build from ~$80,000; all-out performance build ~$120,000; one non-Eleanor Unique Performance build reported at over $180,000 total
- Torque, wheelbase, curb weight: unstated — not found in a citable source
- Evidence: SRC-216, SRC-217
