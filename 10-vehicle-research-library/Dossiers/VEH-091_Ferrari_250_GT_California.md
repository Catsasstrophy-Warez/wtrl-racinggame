## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 2.953L front-longitudinal 60° V12 (Colombo-type, types 128C/128D)
- Power: 240 hp @ 7,000 rpm (SWB, period-quoted; some sources list 6,600 rpm)
- Torque: approx. 190 lb-ft (258 Nm) @ 5,000 rpm (secondary-source figure)
- Transmission: 4-speed manual, all-synchromesh
- Drive layout: RWD, front-engine
- Wheelbase: 2,600 mm (LWB, 1957-1960) / 2,400 mm (SWB, 1960-1963)
- Curb weight: not captured in this pass
- Evidence: SRC-091; SRC-092
