## Cross-reference note (technical-category pass, not a standalone vehicle)
This is a real tuner-culture category, not a single factory vehicle — the Fox-body 5.0 platform (VEH-026) was a common turbo-conversion donor through the 1980s-1990s aftermarket. No invented dyno numbers are used here.

**Real factory turbo reference points (these are NOT aftermarket, but establish Ford's own turbo-4cyl Fox-era baseline, web-verified this pass):**
- 1979-1980 Mustang Cobra: non-intercooled 2.3L turbo four, 132 hp / 143 lb-ft (discontinued 1981 for reliability issues).
- 1983-1984 Turbo GT: intercooled 2.3L turbo four, 145 hp / 180 lb-ft.
- 1984-1986 Mustang SVO: reworked 2.3L turbo four, up to 205 hp / 248 lb-ft by 1985/86.

**Aftermarket 5.0 V8 turbo conversions (period tuner culture, general range only — not a specific documented build):** period and modern turbo kits for the Fox 5.0 (e.g. On3 Performance-style single-turbo systems, a direct descendant of the era's kit-car culture) are commonly documented in the 400-500 rear-wheel-horsepower range on pump gas, with 100+ rwhp gains over stock being a typical single-turbo baseline. This pass did not find a single named, well-documented period (1980s/90s) tuner dyno sheet with an exact hp figure worth citing as a hard spec — the widely-repeated "400-500 whp" range comes from current aftermarket retailer copy (AmericanMuscle) describing what modern kits achieve on this same chassis, not a period-verified specific build. Flagged honestly rather than presented as a hard historical number.

**Cross-reference:** base chassis is VEH-026 (Fox-body Mustang family, itself still an unresearched template — see VEH-150's note); the factory turbo-4 cars above do not have their own dossiers in this library's current 1-159 range.
