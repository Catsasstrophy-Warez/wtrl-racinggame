## Verified specification snapshot
Headline configuration below is the ZR-1 "King of the Hill" performance variant (1990-1995); base C4 small-block (L98/LT1) figures are not independently re-verified in this pass and are left unstated.
- Engine: 5.7L LT5 DOHC 32-valve all-aluminum V8 (Chevrolet/Lotus/Mercury Marine)
- Power: 375 hp @ 5800 rpm (1990-1992); 405 hp (1993-1995, revised heads/valvetrain/cams)
- Torque: 370 lb-ft (1990-1992); 385 lb-ft (1993-1995)
- Transmission: 6-speed manual (ZF)
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: 96.2 in
- Curb weight: 3465 lb
- Measured: 0-60 ~4.5 s; quarter mile ~12.8-13.8 s (period test data, see SRC-078)
- Evidence: SRC-027, SRC-075, SRC-078
