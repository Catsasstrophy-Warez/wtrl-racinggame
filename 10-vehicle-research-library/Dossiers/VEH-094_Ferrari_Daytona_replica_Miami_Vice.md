## Specification status
The "Ferrari Daytona" driven by Sonny Crockett in Miami Vice is a real, well-documented non-Ferrari replica: a fiberglass Ferrari 365 GTB/4 "Daytona" Spyder body built by Tom McBurnie (McBurnie Coachcraft) over a real Chevrolet Corvette C3 chassis/drivetrain. Ferrari's lawsuit against McBurnie over the unlicensed replica is reported as a direct cause of the on-screen car being written out (blown up) in Season 3, after which Ferrari supplied real Testarossas to the production.

## Verified specification snapshot (real donor basis)
- Body: fiberglass Ferrari 365 GTB/4 Daytona Spyder replica body with black plexiglass headlight covers, built by Tom McBurnie / McBurnie Coachcraft
- Donor chassis/drivetrain: Chevrolet Corvette C3 (two cars used across the show's run — "Car One" reportedly based on a 1976 Corvette, "Car Four" reportedly based on a 1981 Corvette)
- Reported engine output: approximately 350 hp (as reported for the show cars; not an independently re-dynoed figure)
- Transmission: 3-speed automatic (as reported for the show cars)
- Note: not a real Ferrari in any respect — chassis, drivetrain, and monocoque are Corvette-derived; only the body/styling references the real Ferrari 365 GTB/4 Daytona (covered separately as the genuine article elsewhere in this library where applicable)
- Evidence: SRC-236, SRC-237
