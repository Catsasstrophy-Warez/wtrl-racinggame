## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 5.7L DOHC 48-valve V12, computer-controlled multi-point fuel injection
- Power: 492 hp
- Torque: approx. 428 lb-ft (580 Nm) (secondary-source figure)
- Transmission: 5-speed manual
- Drive layout: RWD (original 1990 Diablo); AWD introduced later with Diablo VT
- Wheelbase: 2,650 mm (104.3 in)
- Curb weight: approx. 1,576 kg (3,474 lb) (secondary-source figure)
- Evidence: SRC-096; SRC-097
