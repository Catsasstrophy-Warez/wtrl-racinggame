## Verified specification snapshot
1971 was a body redesign (3 in wider) to fit the big-block 429 without further suspension changes. Engine lineup for the SportsRoof/Mach 1:
- Engine options: 302/351 small-block Windsor/Cleveland V8s, and 429 ci / 7.0L "385-series" big-block V8 (429 Cobra Jet / Super Cobra Jet)
- Power: 351 V8 240 hp or 285 hp (285 hp version replaced mid-year by a lower-compression 280 hp 351CJ); 429 Cobra Jet 370 hp, 429 Super Cobra Jet 375 hp
- 429 CJ/SCJ details: 4-bolt mains, forged rods/pistons, 11.3:1 compression, hydraulic cam, aluminum valve covers (429 replaced 428 CJ; no parts interchange with prior FE-family 428)
- Transmission: 4-speed manual or automatic depending on engine
- Drive layout: RWD
- Body: 3 in wider than 1969-70 to package the 429 big-block without major suspension redesign
- Curb weight/wheelbase: unstated to a specific verified figure for this exact configuration
- Evidence: SRC-204, SRC-205
