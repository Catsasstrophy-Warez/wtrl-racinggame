## Cross-reference note (technical-category pass, not a standalone vehicle)
The Fox-body 5.0 LX (1987-1993) was Ford's stripped-trim notchback/hatchback carrying the same 5.0L HO V8 and T5 5-speed as the contemporary Mustang GT, at meaningfully lower curb weight due to reduced trim/glass/interior content — the reason it became the preferred drag-strip/budget-performance choice of the era over the GT.

**2026-09-19 update:** VEH-026 has since been researched in a later pass and
now carries a real "Verified specification snapshot" (5.0 HO: 225hp/300lb-ft
1987-92, 205hp/275lb-ft after the 1993 net-rating change, shared T5 5-speed).
Per that same drivetrain being shared with the LX trim (the point this
cross-reference note already made), those figures now apply directly to
the LX as well for engine/transmission purposes — the remaining gap is
LX-specific curb weight and 1/4-mile figures (the trim's real, lower weight
vs. the GT of the same year), which still has no dedicated source in hand
and is not invented here.

**Honest conclusion:** the drivetrain-sharing figures are now real and
sourced via VEH-026; the LX-specific weight/timing delta remains a genuine,
flagged gap rather than a guessed number.
