## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 8.0L (488 cu in) odd-firing V10, aluminum block/heads (Lamborghini engineering assistance on initial casting design)
- Power: 400 hp @ 4,600 rpm
- Torque: 450-465 lb-ft @ 3,600 rpm (sources vary slightly between 450 and 465 lb-ft)
- Transmission: 6-speed manual (BorgWarner T56)
- Drive layout: front-engine, RWD
- Wheelbase: 96.3 in (2,446 mm)
- Curb weight: 3,285 lb (1,490 kg)
- Evidence: SRC-058
