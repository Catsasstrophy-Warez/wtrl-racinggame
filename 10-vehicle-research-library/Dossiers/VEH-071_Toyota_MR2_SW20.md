## Verified specification snapshot
Snapshot is for the JDM 3S-GTE turbo SW20 (third-generation 3S-GTE, 1993-1999); USDM emissions-tuned Turbo was capped around 200-245 hp depending on model year, and the US-market Turbo was discontinued after 1995.
- Engine: 2.0L 3S-GTE turbocharged DOHC inline-four, mid-mounted
- Power: 245 PS (180 kW / 242 hp) @ 6,000 rpm
- Torque: 304 Nm (224 lb-ft) @ 4,000 rpm
- Transmission: 5-speed manual
- Drive layout: RWD (mid-engine)
- Wheelbase: 2,400 mm
- Curb weight: approx. 1,270 kg
- Evidence: SRC-023, SRC-149
