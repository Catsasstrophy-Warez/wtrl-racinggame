## Verified specification snapshot
Base C6 coupe/convertible configuration (2005-2007 LS2; later years used LS3/LS7 and are a separate sub-configuration).
- Engine: 6.0L LS2 V8
- Power: 400 hp @ 6000 rpm
- Torque: 400 lb-ft @ 4400 rpm
- Transmission: 6-speed manual (T-56) or 4-speed automatic (4L65E, 2005 only)
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: 105.7 in
- Curb weight: 3179 lb (base)
- Evidence: SRC-027, SRC-077
