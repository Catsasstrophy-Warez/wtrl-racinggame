## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 289 ci / 4.7L K-code V8, Holley 715/725-cfm 4-barrel carburetor, aluminum dual-plane intake, Tri-Y tube headers
- Power: 306 hp @ 6,000 rpm
- Torque: 329 lb-ft @ 4,200 rpm
- Transmission: 4-speed manual standard; SelectShift 3-speed automatic newly optional for 1966 (1965 was manual-only)
- Drive layout: RWD, live rear axle
- Wheelbase: 108 in (shared K-code Mustang platform)
- Curb weight: unstated (not sourced to a citable figure specific to 1966)
- Evidence: SRC-200, SRC-201
