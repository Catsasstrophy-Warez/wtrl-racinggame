# VEH-103 — Vision Gran Turismo concepts

**Category:** physics/geometry/presentation reference

**Research type:** real/reference  

## WTRL research role
Use as an engineering, historical, packaging, handling, visual-language, or career-reference input. Real specifications are research evidence, not automatic WTRL canonical truth.

## Specification status
This is a real, ongoing manufacturer concept-car series (Vision Gran Turismo, "VGT") commissioned by Polyphony Digital/Sony for the Gran Turismo video game franchise since 2013. This dossier is a multi-example overview rather than one single vehicle; each manufacturer publishes its own real, independently-sourced technical specifications for its VGT concept.

## Verified specification snapshot (2-3 representative real VGT concepts)

### Mercedes-AMG Vision Gran Turismo (2013)
- Engine: 5.5L twin-turbocharged V8
- Power: 577 hp
- Torque: 590 lb-ft
- Transmission: 7-speed automatic
- Curb weight: approximately 3,053 lb (aluminum spaceframe with carbon-fiber body elements)
- Notable design: gull-wing doors, eight individual exhaust tailpipes
- Evidence: SRC-241, SRC-242

### BMW Vision Gran Turismo (2014)
- Engine: 3.0L turbocharged inline-6 (M TwinPower Turbo)
- Power: 541 hp (404 kW) at 6,200-7,300 rpm
- Torque: 499 lb-ft (680 Nm) at 1,900 rpm
- Transmission: sequential 6-speed, paddle-shifted
- Drive layout: RWD
- Curb weight: approximately 1,180 kg (~2,601 lb), reported 50:50 weight distribution
- Evidence: SRC-243

- Note: figures above are manufacturer-published concept specifications for real, physically-built or digitally-modeled show cars; they are not independently road-tested production figures and should be treated as OEM concept-spec citations
- Evidence: SRC-241, SRC-242, SRC-243
