## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 5.0L Coyote V8 (Gen 2)
- Power: 435 hp @ 6500 rpm (official Ford-announced rating for the all-new S550 GT, July 2014)
- Torque: 400 lb-ft @ 4250 rpm
- Transmission: 6-speed manual standard; 6-speed automatic with paddle shifters optional
- Drive layout: RWD (first independent-rear-suspension Mustang generation)
- Wheelbase: 107.1 in
- Curb weight: 3,705 lb (manual)
- Evidence: SRC-124, SRC-125

First Mustang generation with fully independent rear suspension, a key handling-architecture inflection point versus the live-axle cars (VEH-014 through VEH-032).
