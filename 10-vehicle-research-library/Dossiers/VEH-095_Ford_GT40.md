## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 4.7L (289 ci) small-block V8 (road/street version); larger 7.0L 427 used in Mk II endurance racers per Ford heritage sources
- Power: 335 hp @ 6,000 rpm (289 street-spec Mk I)
- Torque: approx. 329 lb-ft (secondary-source figure)
- Transmission: manual (5-speed ZF transaxle typical of road-going Mk I)
- Drive layout: RWD, mid-engine
- Wheelbase: 95 in (2,413 mm)
- Curb weight: not resolved to a single figure in this pass (secondary sources place Mk I/Mk IIA in the 1,800-2,300 lb range depending on spec)
- Evidence: SRC-029; SRC-095
- Note: this is the real 1964-1969 period GT40, distinct from VEH-096 (Superformance GT40 MKI continuation/replica), which is sourced separately.
