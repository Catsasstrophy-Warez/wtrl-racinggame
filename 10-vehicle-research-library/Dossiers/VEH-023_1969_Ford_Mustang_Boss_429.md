**Category:** Hero/homologation reference

## WTRL research role

## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 429 ci / 7.0L "semi-hemi" NASCAR-homologation big-block V8 (Boss 429/"820" series heads)
- Power: 375 hp (advertised, factory rating); independent 1970-era dyno testing of the hydraulic-lifter version measured closer to ~352 hp
- Torque: 450 lb-ft @ 3400 rpm
- Transmission: 4-speed manual (Toploader)
- Drive layout: RWD (front-engine, longitudinal)
- Wheelbase: not independently sourced in this pass (shared 1969 SportsRoof platform figure of 108 in is commonly cited but not confirmed here)
- Curb weight: 3,870 lb
- Evidence: SRC-115, SRC-116

Built by Kar Kraft to homologate the Boss 429 engine for NASCAR; only 1,359 were produced across 1969-1970.
