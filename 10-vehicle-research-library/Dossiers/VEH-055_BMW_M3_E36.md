- Power: 240 hp @ 6,000 rpm (US 1995 S50B30US); ~321 hp @ 7,400 rpm (Euro S50B32)
- Torque: 225 lb-ft @ 4,250 rpm (US 1995); 236 lb-ft @ 3,800 rpm (US 1996-99)
- Transmission: 5-speed manual (Getrag, US); 6-speed manual optional (Euro); limited-slip differential
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: 106.7 in (2,700 mm)
- Curb weight: 3,384 lb (US 1995 coupe)
- Evidence: SRC-017, SRC-050
