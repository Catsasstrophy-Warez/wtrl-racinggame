## Verified specification snapshot (film-car-specific details beyond the stock XB GT base)
- Base vehicle: 1973 Ford Falcon XB GT Coupe (see VEH-097 for stock factory specs)
- Film-specific modification: non-functional Weiand 6-71-style supercharger scoop added atop the engine for the original 1979 "Mad Max" film (cosmetic only, not functionally driven in that film)
- Builder: modified by Melbourne-based customizer Graf-X International for the film production
- Note: a functional supercharger and further body-kit changes were added for the 1981 sequel "Mad Max 2" (The Road Warrior), a different prop build stage from the first film's car — no independently-cited horsepower figure exists for either film-modified state in sources found this pass
- Evidence: SRC-181 (film-car build history only; hard drivetrain specs remain sourced via VEH-097/SRC-060/SRC-061)
