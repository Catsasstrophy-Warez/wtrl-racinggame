## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: base 200 ci / 3.3L I6; 289 ci / 4.7L V8 (195 hp or 271 hp Hi-Po); new-for-1967 390 ci / 6.4L FE V8
- Power: 120 hp (I6) up to 320 hp @ 4600 rpm (390 V8, GT package)
- Torque: not sourced in this pass
- Transmission: 3-speed manual standard; 4-speed manual, 5-speed manual (later), and Cruise-O-Matic automatic offered across the range
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: 108 in
- Curb weight: 2,758–3,704 lb depending on body/engine configuration
- Evidence: SRC-112

1967 was the first Mustang year to offer the big-block 390 FE V8, a direct precursor to the 1968 GT 390 (VEH-020).
