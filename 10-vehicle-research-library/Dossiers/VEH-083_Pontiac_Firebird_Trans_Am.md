## Verified specification snapshot

- Power: 305 hp
- Torque: 335 lb-ft @ 4,000 rpm
- Transmission: 6-speed manual (T56) or 4-speed automatic
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: 101.1 in
- Curb weight: 3,284 lb (LS1 coupe)
- Evidence: SRC-038, SRC-056, SRC-057
