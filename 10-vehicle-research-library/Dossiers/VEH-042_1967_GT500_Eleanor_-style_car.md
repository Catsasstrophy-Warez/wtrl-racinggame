## Specification status
This dossier is not one specific real film car — it is the generic "Eleanor-style" 1967 GT500 tribute/replica archetype that emerged from post-2000-film customizer culture (distinct from VEH-044, the real 2000 remake's actual hero cars). No single hard-spec row is asserted as "the" Eleanor; instead this captures the real, well-documented specification pattern such tribute builds use.

## Verified specification snapshot (tribute-build archetype pattern, not one exact car)
- Donor basis: genuine 1967 Shelby GT500 preferred by builders (avoiding 1968+ due to differing firewall stampings, shock tower geometry, and brake booster mounting points), or a 1967 Mustang fastback dressed to GT500/Eleanor spec
- Engine: period-correct builds commonly use a rebuilt/upgraded 428 Cobra Jet V8; modern tribute builds are frequently reported with a 351-series small-block or 5.0/351W-based crate engine as a cost/availability substitute
- Transmission: reported tribute-build pattern includes both original-style C6 automatic and modern 5-speed (Tremec) manual conversions
- Chassis/suspension: modern tribute builds commonly add rack-and-pinion steering conversions, coilover suspension, and Wilwood disc brakes over the original drum/recirculating-ball factory setup
- Wheels: 17-inch wheels commonly cited on tribute builds (period GT500 factory wheels were smaller)
- Note: figures above describe the real tribute/replica-building pattern documented by builders and marketplaces, not a single factory or film-verified spec; not independently re-tested
- Evidence: SRC-230, SRC-238
