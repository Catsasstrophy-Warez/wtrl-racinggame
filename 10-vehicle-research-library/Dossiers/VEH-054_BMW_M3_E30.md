## Verified specification snapshot
Snapshot is for the original 1986 E30 M3 (S14B23, pre-Evolution).
- Engine: 2.3L S14B23 DOHC inline-four (4 valves/cyl, dual throttle bodies)
- Power: 200 PS / 197 bhp (147 kW) @ 6,750 rpm
- Torque: 240 Nm (177 lb-ft) @ 4,750 rpm
- Transmission: 5-speed manual (Getrag 265)
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: 2,562-2,570 mm
- Curb weight: approx. 1,165-1,200 kg
- Evidence: SRC-017, SRC-145
