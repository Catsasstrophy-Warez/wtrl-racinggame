## Verified specification snapshot (real mechanical basis, not the fictional Ferrari)
- Engine: 302 cu in (4.9L) Ford Windsor V8 (donor engine originally from a 1974 Ford Torino), factory-rated 135 hp when new; the replica's rebuilt unit's output is not independently re-tested/cited
- Power: not independently re-certified for the replica; see engine note above
- Torque: not captured with a single citable figure in this pass
- Transmission: Ford 3-speed automatic (reported production choice — actor Matthew Broderick was said to be uncomfortable with a manual)
- Drive layout: RWD, live rear axle with coilover suspension
- Wheelbase/chassis: fabricated steel-tube frame, fiberglass body reportedly derived from an MG body shape and reworked with Ferrari 250 GT California-style styling cues (hood scoops, fender vents, raked windshield)
- Curb weight: not captured with a single citable figure in this pass
- Note: this dossier is the real Modena Design & Development replica's actual mechanical basis, distinct from the fictional Ferrari 250 GT California covered as VEH-091
- Evidence: SRC-180
