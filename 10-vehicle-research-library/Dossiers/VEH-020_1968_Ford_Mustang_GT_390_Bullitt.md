## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 390 ci / 6.4L FE "Thunderbird Special" V8
- Power: 325 hp (factory GT rating; some period sources cite up to ~330 hp)
- Torque: 427 lb-ft @ 3200 rpm
- Transmission: 4-speed manual (film car); 3-speed automatic also offered
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: 108 in (shared GT fastback platform figure)
- Curb weight: ~2,800 lb (GT fastback, 390)
- Evidence: SRC-113, SRC-114
