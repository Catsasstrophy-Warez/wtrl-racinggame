## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 2.568L RB26DETT twin-turbo I6
- Power: 280 PS (206 kW) @ 6800 rpm
- Torque: 392 Nm (40.0 kgm) @ 4400 rpm
- Transmission: 6MT (road-car GT-R; all R34 GT-Rs sold with manual only)
- Drive layout: ATTESA E-TS AWD
- Wheelbase: 2665 mm
- Curb weight: 1560 kg
- Tire: 245/40ZR18
- Evidence: SRC-043, SRC-071
