## Cross-reference note (technical-category pass, not a standalone vehicle)
The 4.6L 3-valve SOHC modular V8 is the 2005-2010 S197 Mustang GT's engine, and **VEH-032 (2005 Mustang GT / S197)** already carries a full "Verified specification snapshot" for it from a prior research pass:
- Power: 300 hp @ 5750 rpm
- Torque: 320 lb-ft @ 4500 rpm
- Transmission: 5-speed manual (Tremec) standard, 5-speed automatic optional
- Curb weight: ~3,450 lb
- Evidence: SRC-123 (automobile-catalog.com)
- Plus separately-cited measured performance data (0-60 5.2s, 1/4 mile 13.8s @ 102mph, 70-0 183ft, 0.87g skidpad) in `Matrices/CALIBRATION-GRADE-DETAIL-WAVE2.csv`.

**Conclusion: this category dossier needs nothing beyond what VEH-032 already covers.** The 4.6L 3V engine did not meaningfully change output across its full 2005-2010 run in the base GT (later 2010 models saw minor gains from intake/head revisions, but this pass did not find a specific, differently-sourced later-year figure worth adding as a separate hard spec). Treat VEH-032 as the single source of truth for this engine family; this dossier is a pointer to it, not independent data.
