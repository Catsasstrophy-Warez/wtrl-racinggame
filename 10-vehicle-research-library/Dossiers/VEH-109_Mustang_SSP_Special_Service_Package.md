## Verified specification snapshot (late-production SSP, e.g. 1989-1993 CHP/state-patrol spec)
- Engine: 302 cu in (5.0L) HO V8
- Power: 225 hp (late-production rating; earlier SSP years were rated 175 hp then 200 hp as the 5.0 HO evolved)
- Torque: ~300 lb-ft
- Transmission: 5-speed manual (T5), with 4-speed manual optional/standard in earliest 1982-83 CHP cars; automatic offered to some agencies
- Drive layout: RWD, limited-slip differential
- Wheelbase: shared with contemporary Fox-body Mustang GT (not independently re-verified this pass)
- Curb weight: not captured with a single citable figure in this pass
- Evidence: SRC-009, SRC-010, SRC-179
