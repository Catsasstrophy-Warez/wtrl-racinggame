## Specification status
The car depicted on screen as a "Boss 429" is real-world identified by automotive press as actually being a 1969 Ford Mustang Mach 1 with its factory graphics/decals removed and restyled — not a genuine Boss 429. Identifying evidence includes the automatic transmission (all real Boss 429s were 4-speed manual only), the presence of hood tie-downs and a rear spoiler (Mach 1-specific factory options not available on the Boss 429), the absence of "BOSS 429" fender decals, and paint colors (Hitman Gray/Gun Metal Gray) not among the 5 factory colors offered on the real Boss 429.

## Verified specification snapshot (real donor basis: 1969 Mustang Mach 1)
- Real donor/base model: 1969 Ford Mustang Mach 1 (per real Car and Driver period test of the Mach 1, filed under VEH family reference): 428 cu in (7.0L) V8, 335 hp, 440 lb-ft torque, 108-in wheelbase, 3,607 lb as tested
- Transmission as shown on screen: automatic (confirmed inconsistent with real factory Boss 429, which was 4-speed manual only)
- Production note: reportedly five cars were built/used for filming and all were destroyed during production, so the exact individual specification of the filming cars cannot be independently verified
- Reference-only (not the film car): genuine 1969 Boss 429 factory rating was 375 hp and 450 lb-ft, 4-speed manual only, in one of 859 units produced
- Evidence: SRC-234, SRC-235, SRC-003 (existing)
