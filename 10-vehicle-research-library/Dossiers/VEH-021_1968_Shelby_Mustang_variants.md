## Verified specification snapshot
1968 brought three distinct Shelby Mustang variants, all on the 108 in wheelbase Mustang platform:
- **GT350 (1968):** 302 ci / 4.9L small-block V8 (replacing the 289), Shelby-rated at 250 hp; 4-speed manual or automatic; RWD
- **GT500 (early 1968, pre-April):** 428 ci / 7.0L Police Interceptor-based V8, factory-rated 360 hp
- **GT500KR "King of the Road" (from April 1968):** 428 ci Cobra Jet V8 replaces the earlier 428, factory-rated 335 hp @ 5,200 rpm / 440 lb-ft @ 3,400 rpm (widely regarded by period sources as understated vs. real-world output well above 400 hp); 735 cfm 4-barrel Holley; 4-speed manual or C6 3-speed automatic; RWD
- Curb weight/exact wheelbase: unstated beyond the shared 108 in Mustang platform figure already cited for other 1967-69 Shelby dossiers
- Evidence: SRC-202, SRC-203
