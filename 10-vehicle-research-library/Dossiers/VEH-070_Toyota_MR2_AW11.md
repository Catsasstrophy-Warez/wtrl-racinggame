## Verified specification snapshot
Snapshot uses Toyota's own JDM reference figures for the first-generation MR2 (AW11); export markets (US/UK) with lower-compression/emissions tuning varied (approx. 122 bhp/91 kW).
- Engine: 1.6L 4A-GE DOHC 16V inline-four, mid-mounted
- Power: 130 PS (JDM reference, per Toyota); export markets approx. 122 bhp (91 kW) @ 6,600 rpm
- Torque: 142 Nm (105 lb-ft) @ 5,000 rpm (export-market figure)
- Transmission: 5-speed manual
- Drive layout: RWD (mid-engine)
- Wheelbase: 2,320 mm
- Curb weight: 950 kg (Toyota JDM reference); export-market figures around 975 kg
- Evidence: SRC-023, SRC-024, SRC-148
