## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 302 ci / 4.9L Windsor V8, SVT-tuned (GT-40 heads/intake)
- Power: 235 hp (factory SVT rating); some technical-archive sources list a 240 bhp @ 4800 rpm dyno-basis figure
- Torque: 280 lb-ft @ 4000 rpm
- Transmission: 5-speed manual (T5)
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: 100.5 in (Fox platform, shared with VEH-027)
- Curb weight: not independently confirmed to the pound in this pass (period sources place it modestly above the base GT's ~3,142 lb)
- Evidence: SRC-120, SRC-121

First-generation SVT Cobra; the only Fox-body year for this model, immediately preceding the SN95-based 1994-95 Cobra and the 1996-98 4.6 DOHC Cobra (VEH-030).
