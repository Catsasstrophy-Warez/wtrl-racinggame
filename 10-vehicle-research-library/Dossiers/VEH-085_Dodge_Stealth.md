## Verified specification snapshot
Snapshot is for the Dodge Stealth R/T Twin Turbo (Mitsubishi-built platform-mate of the 3000GT VR-4); 1991-93 models were rated 300 hp, 1994-95 models 320 hp with revised engine management.
- Engine: 3.0L DOHC twin-turbocharged V6
- Power: 300 hp (1991-93) / 320 hp (1994-95) @ 5,500 rpm
- Torque: 307 lb-ft (1991-93) / 315 lb-ft / 427 Nm (1994-95)
- Transmission: 5-speed manual or 4-speed automatic
- Drive layout: AWD
- Wheelbase: 2,470 mm
- Curb weight: approx. 1,690 kg (3,726 lb)
- Evidence: SRC-152
