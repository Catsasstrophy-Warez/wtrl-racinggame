- Torque: 395 lb-ft (5.7L HEMI, AWD-only rating); 260 lb-ft (3.6L V6)
- Transmission: 5-speed automatic (TorqueFlite) with column shift and manual up/down switch
- Drive layout: RWD or AWD (3.07 rear axle ratio standard with AWD); pursuit-tuned suspension, upgraded brakes, load-leveling shocks
- Wheelbase: not captured in this pass
- Curb weight: not captured in this pass
