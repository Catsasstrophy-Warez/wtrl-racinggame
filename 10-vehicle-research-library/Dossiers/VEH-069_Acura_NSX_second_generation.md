## Verified specification snapshot
Covers the NC1 second-generation NSX (2016/2017-2022).
- Engine: 3.5L twin-turbo DOHC V6 + three-motor Sport Hybrid SH-AWD system (direct-drive motor plus twin-motor front unit)
- Power: 573 hp (system total)
- Torque: 476 lb-ft (system total)
- Transmission: 9-speed dual-clutch (9DCT)
- Drive layout: Sport Hybrid Super Handling All-Wheel Drive (SH-AWD)
- Wheelbase: 103.5 in
- Curb weight: 3803 lb
- Evidence: SRC-022, SRC-074
