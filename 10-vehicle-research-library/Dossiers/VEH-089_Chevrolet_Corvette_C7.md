## Verified specification snapshot (2014 Corvette Stingray, base C7)
- Engine: 6.2L LT1 naturally aspirated V8, direct injection, Active Fuel Management, variable valve timing
- Power: 455 hp (460 hp with optional NPP dual-mode performance exhaust)
- Torque: 460 lb-ft (465 lb-ft with NPP)
- Transmission: 7-speed manual with Active Rev Matching, or 6-speed paddle-shift automatic
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: 106.7 in
- Curb weight: 3,347 lb (base coupe)
- Evidence: SRC-027, SRC-174
