## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 351 cu in (5.8L) Cleveland V8 (locally built by Ford Australia at Geelong); 302 cu in (4.9L) Cleveland V8 also offered across the range
- Power: approximately 260 hp / 194 kW (period Australian test figures for the 351 V8 XB, per automobile-catalog; Ford Australia did not publish a single universally-cited factory hp figure for this pass)
- Torque: not captured in this pass
- Transmission: 4-speed manual (floor-shift) or 3-speed automatic (Cruise-O-Matic)
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: 111.0 in (2,819 mm)
- Curb weight: 3,073 lb (1,394 kg)
- Evidence: SRC-060, SRC-061
