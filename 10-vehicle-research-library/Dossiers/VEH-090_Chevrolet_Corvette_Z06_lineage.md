## Verified specification snapshot (2015 Corvette Z06, C7 generation)
- Engine: 6.2L LT4 supercharged V8, dry-sump, titanium intake valves, 10:1 compression
- Power: 650 hp @ 6,400 rpm (SAE-certified)
- Torque: 650 lb-ft @ 3,600 rpm
- Transmission: 7-speed manual or 8L90 8-speed automatic with paddle shifters, carbon-fiber torque tube
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: 106.7 in
- Curb weight: 3,524 lb (coupe, per GM's own order-guide figure)
- Evidence: SRC-028, SRC-175, SRC-176
