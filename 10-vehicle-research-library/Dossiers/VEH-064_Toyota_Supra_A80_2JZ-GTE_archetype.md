## Verified specification snapshot
The filename says "archetype," but the exact vehicle is resolvable: this is the real 1993-2002 Toyota Supra (A80), 2JZ-GTE twin-turbo. Two distinct market configurations are verified; do not blend them into a single invented number.
- JDM launch spec (1993 Toyota Global press materials): 3.0L twin-turbo 2JZ-GTE I6, 280 PS @ 5600 rpm, 44.0 kgm (431 Nm) @ 3600 rpm, Getrag 6MT (or ECT-iS automatic), RWD, electronic throttle control (ETCS)
- US/Canada Turbo spec (period road test, 1993 model year): 320 hp @ 5600 rpm, 315 lb-ft @ 4000 rpm, 6-speed manual, RWD, ~3450-3600 lb as-tested weight range
- Measured (US Turbo, period test): 0-60 mph ~4.6-5.2 s (varies by test/gearing), quarter mile ~13.1-13.8 s, 70-0 braking ~160 ft
- Evidence: SRC-041, SRC-072
