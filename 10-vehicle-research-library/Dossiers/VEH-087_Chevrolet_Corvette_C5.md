## Verified specification snapshot
Base C5 coupe/convertible configuration (1997-2000 LS1; output rose to 350 hp/365 lb-ft from 2001 with revised intake/exhaust — treat as a distinct sub-configuration).
- Engine: 5.7L LS1 V8
- Power: 345 hp @ 5600 rpm (1997-2000)
- Torque: 350 lb-ft @ 4400 rpm (1997-2000)
- Transmission: 6-speed manual (T-56) or 4-speed automatic (4L60E)
- Drive layout: RWD, rear transaxle, 50/50 front-rear weight distribution
- Wheelbase: 104.5 in
- Curb weight: 3245 lb
- Evidence: SRC-027, SRC-076
