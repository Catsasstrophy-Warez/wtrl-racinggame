## Verified specification snapshot
Snapshot is for the JDM-only Honda Civic Type R (EK9), 1997-2000.
- Engine: 1.6L B16B DOHC VTEC inline-four (hand-ported)
- Power: 185 PS (136 kW / 182 hp) @ 8,200 rpm
- Torque: 160 Nm (118 lb-ft) @ 7,500 rpm
- Transmission: 5-speed close-ratio manual, front helical LSD
- Drive layout: FWD
- Wheelbase: 2,620 mm
- Curb weight: approx. 1,090 kg (2,403 lb)
- Evidence: SRC-146
