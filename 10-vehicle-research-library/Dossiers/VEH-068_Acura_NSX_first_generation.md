## Verified specification snapshot
Covers the NA1/NA2 first-generation NSX (1991-2005). Two headline engine/output configurations are OEM-verified; later-model curb weight/wheelbase changes (e.g. NA2 widened rear track) are not yet independently confirmed in this library and are left unstated.
- Engine (1991 launch): 3.0L C30A DOHC VTEC V6, all-aluminum monocoque
- Power (1991): 270 hp; Torque (1991): 210 lb-ft
- Engine (2002 manual): 3.2L C32B DOHC VTEC V6
- Power (2002 manual): 290 hp @ 7100 rpm; Torque (2002 manual): 224 lb-ft @ 5500 rpm (8000 rpm redline)
- Transmission: 5MT or 4AT (1991); 6MT (3.2 manual) or 4AT (3.0 automatic) in later years
- Drive layout: RWD, mid-engine
- Wheelbase: 99.6 in
- Evidence: SRC-020, SRC-021, SRC-073
