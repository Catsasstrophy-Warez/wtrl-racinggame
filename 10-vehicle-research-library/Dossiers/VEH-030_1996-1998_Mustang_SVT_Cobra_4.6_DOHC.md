## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 4.6L / 281 ci DOHC 32-valve modular V8
- Power: 305 hp @ 5800 rpm
- Torque: 300 lb-ft @ 4800 rpm
- Transmission: 5-speed manual (T-45)
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: not independently confirmed in this pass (SN95 platform; commonly cited at 101.3 in but not sourced here)
- Curb weight: 3,391 lb (coupe); 3,531 lb (convertible)
- Evidence: SRC-122

First application of Ford's DOHC 32-valve modular V8 in a Mustang, marking the transition away from the pushrod 5.0/5.8 Windsor family used in VEH-027/VEH-028 toward the modular architecture that carries through the S197/S550 generations (VEH-032, VEH-033).
