## Identity note
"M3 GTR" refers to a genuinely distinct real vehicle, not F80/G80 trim badges (BMW never sold an F80/G80 "GTR"). It is the E46-generation M3 GTR: a homologation-driven ALMS GT-class race car (2001, Schnitzer Motorsport) powered by BMW's naturally aspirated P60B40 4.0L V8, plus a small run of road-legal "Strassenversion" homologation cars BMW built in 2001 to satisfy GT racing rules after rivals protested the racing engine's dominance.

## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: P60B40 4.0L (3,997 cc) naturally aspirated 90° V8, dry sump, flat-plane crank, forged internals
- Power (Strassenversion road car): 350 hp
- Power (ALMS race car, restrictor-limited): approximately 444-500 hp depending on restrictor configuration; unrestricted dyno figures cited up to 650-700 hp
- Torque (Strassenversion): 365 Nm (269 lb-ft) @ 5,000 rpm
- Transmission: 6-speed manual (Strassenversion); sequential racing transmission on the ALMS car
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: not captured in this pass
- Curb weight: 1,350 kg (Strassenversion)
- Evidence: SRC-044, SRC-045, SRC-055
