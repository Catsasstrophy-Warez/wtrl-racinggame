## Specification status

## Verified specification snapshot (representative real generations)

### 911 (996) generation (1997-2005, first water-cooled 911)
- First 911 generation with a water-cooled engine and non-round headlights
- Drag coefficient: Cd 0.30
- Total production: 175,262 units across the generation (multiple body styles/powertrains)
- Evidence: SRC-246

### 911 GT3 RS (current/modern generation)
- Power: 518 hp
- Evidence: SRC-013

- Note: intermediate water-cooled generations (997, 991, 992) are not separately re-verified with hard figures in this pass; use OEM Porsche Newsroom press kits per-generation for further entries before treating any intermediate figure as canonical
- Evidence: SRC-013, SRC-246
