## Verified specification snapshot
GT500CR is a real, distinct commercial continuation/restomod product built by Classic Recreations (1967/1968 Mustang-based, officially licensed), separate from both Ford factory Mustangs and Shelby American's own products.
- Engine range: Ford Performance Gen III/IV Coyote 5.0L V8-based lineup, spanning 480 hp to 825 hp depending on configuration
- Carbon Fiber Edition: 5.2L Coyote (Gen 3) + 2.9L Whipple supercharger, 810 hp / 600 lb-ft
- High-performance variant: tuned Gen 3 Coyote 5.0L + Magnuson VMP supercharger, 800 hp, claimed 0-60 in 3.2 sec
- Transmission: Ford Performance 10R80 10-speed automatic or Tremec T-56 SuperMagnum 6-speed manual
- Drive layout: RWD; Detroit Speed Quadralink rear suspension
- Brakes: Wilwood 14-in 6-piston slotted/vented discs
- Wheelbase/curb weight: unstated to a specific citation in this pass
- Evidence: SRC-036, SRC-214, SRC-215
