## Specification status
This is a real Toyota Gazoo Racing show concept (a track-focused racing derivative of the Toyota S-FR concept), publicly shown at the 2016 Tokyo Auto Salon. Toyota did not publish a full independently-verified powertrain output figure for this specific racing concept; the closest sourced figures are for the related road-going S-FR concept's rumored production-derived engine.

## Verified specification snapshot
- Concept type: track/racing-styled derivative of the Toyota S-FR lightweight sports car concept, developed/shown by Toyota Gazoo Racing (TGR) at Tokyo Auto Salon 2016
- Engine: exact output not officially disclosed by Toyota for the racing concept; press speculation at the time pointed to a supercharged version of a small-displacement engine, distinct from the road S-FR concept's rumored 1.2L turbo I3 (approx. 115 PS/185 Nm, sourced from Euro-spec Auris) or 1.5L NA I4 (approx. 130 PS/148 Nm) candidates — none of these figures are confirmed as fitted to the racing concept specifically
- Body/aero: carbon-fiber-reinforced plastic (CFRP) front splitter, canards, and rear spoiler; vented hood; center-lock wheels
- Note: leave power/torque/transmission/weight fields blank for this specific concept pending an official Toyota technical-spec release; treat this dossier's numbers as unconfirmed press speculation, not verified spec
- Evidence: SRC-239, SRC-240
