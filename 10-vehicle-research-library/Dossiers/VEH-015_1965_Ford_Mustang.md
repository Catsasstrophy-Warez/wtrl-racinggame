## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: base 200 ci / 3.3L I6; 289 ci / 4.7L V8 offered in 2bbl (200 hp), 4bbl (225 hp), and Hi-Po (271 hp) tunes
- Power: 120 hp (I6) up to 271 hp @ 6000 rpm (289 Hi-Po V8)
- Torque: not sourced in this pass
- Transmission: 3-speed manual standard; 4-speed manual and 3-speed automatic optional
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: 108 in
- Curb weight: ~2,562 lb (hardtop coupe, base I6)
- Evidence: SRC-111

Distinct from VEH-016 (1965 Shelby GT350), this is the standard/base production 1965 Mustang.
