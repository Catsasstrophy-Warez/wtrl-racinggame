## Verified specification snapshot
RUF is an independent, VDA/BMBF-recognized German manufacturer (Pfaffenhausen) that builds its own vehicles on/using Porsche 911 architecture; the following is for its best-known model, the RUF CTR ("Yellowbird," 1987).
- Engine: 3.4L twin-turbocharged flat-six
- Power: 469 PS (345 kW / 463 hp)
- Torque: 553 Nm (408 lb-ft)
- Transmission: 5-speed manual
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: 2,273 mm
- Curb weight: 1,150 kg (2,535 lb)
- Evidence: SRC-144
