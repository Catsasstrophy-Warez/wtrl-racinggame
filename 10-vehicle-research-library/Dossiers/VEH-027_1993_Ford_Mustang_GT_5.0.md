## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 302 ci / 5.0L HO Windsor V8
- Power: 205 hp
- Torque: 275 lb-ft
- Transmission: 5-speed manual (T5) standard; 4-speed AOD automatic optional
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: 100.5 in (Fox platform)
- Curb weight: ~3,142 lb (1,426 kg)
- Evidence: SRC-119, SRC-010

Final model year of the Fox-body 5.0 before the 1994 SN95 redesign; direct precursor context for the 1993 SVT Cobra (VEH-028), which shares this chassis and engine family in a higher state of tune.
