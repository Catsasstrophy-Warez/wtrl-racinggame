- Power: 215 bhp @ 6,000 rpm (1987 Turbo Esprit HC)
- Torque: 220 lb-ft @ 4,250 rpm
- Transmission: 5-speed manual
- Drive layout: RWD, mid-engine
- Wheelbase: not captured in this pass
- Curb weight: not captured in this pass
- Evidence: SRC-090
- Note: the later Esprit Turbo SE (1989-1993) used a charge-cooled version of the same 2.174L four rated at 264 hp / 261 lb-ft; that variant is a distinct configuration and not asserted here without independent confirmation.
