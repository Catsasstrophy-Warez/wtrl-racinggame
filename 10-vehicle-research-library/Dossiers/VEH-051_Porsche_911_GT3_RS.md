## Verified specification snapshot
Snapshot below is for the 997-generation 911 GT3 RS 4.0 (2011), the highest-output naturally aspirated 911 GT3 RS at time of writing and the configuration with the clearest citable OEM press-kit data.
- Engine: 4.0L naturally aspirated flat-six (crankshaft derived from 911 GT3 RSR racing engine, titanium con-rods)
- Power: 500 PS (368 kW) @ 8,250 rpm
- Torque: 460 Nm (340 lb-ft) @ 5,750 rpm
- Transmission: 6-speed manual
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: not captured in this pass (997-generation range is 2,350-2,360 mm; not confirmed for this exact variant)
- Curb weight: 1,360 kg (with fluids, per Porsche press kit)
- Evidence: SRC-013, SRC-141, SRC-142
