## Verified specification snapshot
The SN95 generation (1994-2004, covering both the original SN95 and New Edge facelift) transitioned from the pushrod 5.0L to the modular 4.6L SOHC V8 for the GT:
- 1994-1995 GT: 5.0L (302 ci) pushrod V8 carried over, 215 hp / 285 lb-ft
- 1996-1997 GT: new 4.6L SOHC 2-valve modular V8 replaces the 5.0L, 215 hp / 285 lb-ft
- 1998 GT: 4.6L SOHC 2-valve, 225 hp / 290 lb-ft
- 1999-2004 GT (New Edge facelift, PI heads): 4.6L SOHC 2-valve, 260 hp / 302 lb-ft
- Transmission: 5-speed manual (T-45/Tremec 3650) or 4-speed automatic
- Drive layout: RWD, solid rear axle
- Wheelbase/curb weight: unstated to a specific citation in this pass
- Evidence: SRC-208, SRC-209
