## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 1.8L 1ZZ-FE DOHC 16V inline-four, mid-mounted
- Power: 140 PS (Toyota JDM reference); US-market rated 138 hp @ 6,400 rpm
- Torque: 170 Nm (125 lb-ft) @ 4,400 rpm
- Transmission: 5-speed manual (later 6-speed sequential manual transmission option)
- Drive layout: RWD (mid-engine)
- Wheelbase: 2,450 mm
- Curb weight: approx. 970 kg (Toyota reference); US Spyder curb weight approx. 995 kg (2,195 lb)
- Evidence: SRC-023, SRC-025, SRC-150
