## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 2.926L (2926cc) naturally aspirated 90° V8 (308 GTB, carbureted early series)
- Power: 255 bhp @ 7,500 rpm
- Torque: approx. 210 lb-ft @ 5,000 rpm (secondary-source figure)
- Transmission: 5-speed manual
- Drive layout: RWD, mid-engine, with limited-slip differential
- Wheelbase: 2,340 mm (92.1 in)
- Curb weight: approx. 1,100 kg (2,425 lb) (secondary-source figure)
- Evidence: SRC-098; SRC-099
