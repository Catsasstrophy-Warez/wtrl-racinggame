## Verified specification snapshot (representative real generations)

### 911 Carrera RS 2.7 (1973, "F-series")
- Engine: 2,687 cc air-cooled flat-six
- Power: 210 hp
- Top speed: 245 km/h
- Evidence: SRC-011

### 911 Turbo 3.3 / 930 (1978-1989)
- Engine: 3.3L turbocharged flat-six
- Power: 300 PS
- Torque: 412 Nm
- Top speed: 260 km/h
- Evidence: SRC-012 (see also VEH-048 for full single-vehicle dossier)

### 911 (964) generation (1989-1994)
- Naturally aspirated Carrera: 3.6L flat-six, 250 PS, up to 260 km/h
- 964 Turbo (1990-1992): 3.3L turbo flat-six, 320 PS (Porsche Exclusive tune up to 355 PS; Turbo S up to 381 PS)
- 964 Turbo (1993+): 3.6L turbo flat-six, 360 PS
- 964 Carrera RS 3.8 (1993): 3.8L naturally aspirated flat-six, 300 PS
- Evidence: SRC-244

### 911 (993) generation (1993-1998, final air-cooled generation)
- Six-cylinder boxer engine power outputs ranged from 272 PS (Carrera variants) to 450 PS (911 Turbo S, 1998, and Turbo with performance pack)
- Body styles: Coupe (from autumn 1993), Cabriolet (early 1994), Targa (1995, new retractable glass-roof concept), Carrera 4 AWD variant from 1995
- Evidence: SRC-245
