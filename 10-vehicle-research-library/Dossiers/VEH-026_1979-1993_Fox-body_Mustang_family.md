## Verified specification snapshot
Representative real hard specs for the 5.0L HO V8 across the Fox-body era (as a family envelope, not one exact configuration):
- Engine: 5.0L (302 ci) HO small-block V8, roller-cam from 1985
- Power/Torque: 225 hp / 300 lb-ft (net) for 1987-1992 5.0 HO Mustang GT/LX; Ford's own 1993 rating dropped to 205 hp / 275 lb-ft for the same basic engine (widely attributed to a recalibrated/more conservative rating rather than a hardware change)
- Transmission: Borg-Warner T5 5-speed manual or AOD 4-speed automatic
- Drive layout: RWD, solid rear axle (8.8 in from 1986)
- Wheelbase: unstated to a specific citation in this pass
- Curb weight: unstated to a specific citation in this pass
- Evidence: SRC-206, SRC-207 (in addition to existing SRC-009/SRC-010)
