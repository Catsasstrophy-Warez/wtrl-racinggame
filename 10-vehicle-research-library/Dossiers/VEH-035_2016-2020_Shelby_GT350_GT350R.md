## Verified specification snapshot (2016-2020 GT350 base)
- Engine: 5.2L "Voodoo" flat-plane-crank V8, naturally aspirated
- Power: 526 hp @ 7,500 rpm
- Torque: 429 lb-ft @ 4,750 rpm (redline 8,250 rpm, 12.0:1 compression)
- Transmission: TR-3160 6-speed manual (Tremec-based), twin-disc clutch
- Drive layout: RWD, Torsen limited-slip
- Wheelbase: 107.1 in
- Curb weight: ~3,760 lb (base coupe; Track Pack and R differ — see below)
- Evidence: SRC-005, SRC-170

## GT350R variant deltas (2016-2020, distinct track configuration)
- Same 5.2L Voodoo V8/power/torque as base GT350 (526 hp / 429 lb-ft); no factory power increase for R
- Transmission/drive as base GT350 (TR-3160 6MT, Torsen LSD, RWD)
- Distinguishing hardware: Carbon Revolution one-piece carbon-fiber wheels (18 lb front/rear vs. ~33 lb aluminum equivalent), Michelin Pilot Sport Cup 2 tires (305/30ZR19 front, 315/30ZR19 rear), no rear seat, no A/C standard, adjustable front splitter and rear wing
- Curb weight: 3,650 lb as announced by Ford at the GT350R media event (Grattan Raceway), >130 lb lighter than a GT350 with Track Package
- Evidence: SRC-171, SRC-172
