## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 302 ci / 4.9L Windsor V8 (1978 King Cobra, top emissions-era option; base cars offered a 2.3L I4 and 2.8L V6, not independently sourced here)
- Power: 139 hp (SAE net, 1978 302 V8 King Cobra)
- Torque: 250 lb-ft
- Transmission: 4-speed manual
- Drive layout: RWD
- Wheelbase: 96.2 in
- Curb weight: 3,153 lb
- Evidence: SRC-117, SRC-118

The Mustang II marks the emissions-era low point in the lineage's power-to-weight curve, useful as a contrast reference against the earlier big-block cars (VEH-020, VEH-023) and the later Fox-body recovery (VEH-027).
