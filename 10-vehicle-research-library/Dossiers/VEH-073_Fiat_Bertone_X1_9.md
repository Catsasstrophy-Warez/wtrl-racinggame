## Verified specification snapshot
Snapshot is for the Bertone-built, North American-market 1.5L X1/9 (1984-1989 federalized SAE-net figures); European-market 1.5L cars were rated higher (85 bhp) prior to US emissions tuning.
- Engine: 1.5L SOHC inline-four, mid-mounted
- Power: 76 PS / 75 hp (56 kW) @ 6,000 rpm (US SAE net); European 1.5L rated 85 bhp @ 6,000 rpm
- Torque: 108 Nm (80 lb-ft) (US SAE net); European 1.5L rated 118 Nm (87 lb-ft) @ 3,200 rpm
- Transmission: 5-speed manual
- Drive layout: RWD (mid-engine)
- Wheelbase: 2,202 mm
- Curb weight: 880-920 kg
- Evidence: SRC-035, SRC-151
