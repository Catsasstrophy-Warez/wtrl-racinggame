## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 3.2L (Tipo F105CB) naturally aspirated 90° V8
- Power: 270 hp
- Torque: approx. 231 lb-ft (secondary-source figure)
- Transmission: 5-speed manual
- Drive layout: RWD, mid-engine
- Wheelbase: 2,350 mm (92.5 in)
- Curb weight: approx. 1,263 kg (2,784 lb) (secondary-source figure)
- Evidence: SRC-100; SRC-101
