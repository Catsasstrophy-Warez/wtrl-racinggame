## Verified specification snapshot
Snapshot is for the Acura RSX Type-S (K20A2), 2002-2006.
- Engine: 2.0L K20A2 DOHC i-VTEC inline-four (VTEC on both intake and exhaust cams)
- Power: 200 hp (149 kW) @ 7,400 rpm
- Torque: 142 lb-ft (193 Nm) @ 6,000 rpm
- Transmission: 6-speed close-ratio manual
- Drive layout: FWD
- Wheelbase: 101.2 in (2,570 mm)
- Curb weight: approx. 2,840 lb (1,288 kg)
- Evidence: SRC-147
