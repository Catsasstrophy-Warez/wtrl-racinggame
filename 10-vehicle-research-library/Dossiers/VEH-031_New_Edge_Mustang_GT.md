**Category:** Hero reference

## Verified specification snapshot
- Engine: 4.6L (281 ci) SOHC 2-valve modular V8 with redesigned "PI" (Performance Improved) heads — new intake ports, combustion chambers, and camshaft profiles for 1999, a roughly 45 hp gain over the 1996-98 NPI-head engine
- Power: 260 hp
- Torque: 302 lb-ft
- Redline: 5,800 rpm
- Transmission: 5-speed manual (Tremec 3650) standard; 4-speed automatic optional
- Drive layout: RWD, solid rear axle
- Reported 0-60 mph: ~5.4 sec (1999 model, period test/enthusiast-sourced, not an OEM figure)
- Curb weight/wheelbase: unstated to a specific citation in this pass
- These 260 hp/302 lb-ft figures were consistent across the full 1999-2004 New Edge GT run
- Evidence: SRC-210, SRC-211
