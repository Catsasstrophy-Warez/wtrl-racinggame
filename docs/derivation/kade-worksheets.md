## 1. What is the response?
The class's most aerodynamically refined period: smoothed, curved body
panels replacing the 80s' boxiness, flush composite headlamps, a larger
functional hood scoop reflecting bigger turbo hardware, and — in top
variants across the real class — a large rear wing on twin risers.

# Derivation Worksheet: Kade — 2000s

Reference:        Japanese turbocharged inline-six coupe class, late era
Domain:           Vehicle model
Date:             production-pending
Target in game:   Kade, era 3 of 3

---

## 1. What is the response?
The class's final refinement before this architecture generally exits
production across the real world (`30` §3.3 — Kade does not survive
into the 2010s): sharper crease lines replacing the 90s' soft curves,
projector headlamps, larger front intakes for increasingly powerful
turbo hardware, and a more aggressive splitter treatment.

## 2. Feature inventory
1. Sharper body crease lines
2. Projector headlamp units
3. Larger, more aggressive front intake openings
4. Front splitter treatment
5. Integrated rear wing (less prominent than 90s twin-riser style)
6. Larger diameter wheels, lower-profile tyres
7. Twin exhaust tips, larger bore
8. More pronounced front fender vents

| # | Feature | Protected? | Why |

| 4 | Front splitter | No | Functional aero, industry-standard |
| 5 | Integrated wing | No | Common packaging solution |
| 6 | Larger wheels/lower tyres | No | An industry-wide fitment trend |
| 7 | Larger exhaust bore | No | Functional, scales with output |

why `30` §3.3 has Kade exit the lineage here rather than continuing.
