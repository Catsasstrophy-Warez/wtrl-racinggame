# Derivation Worksheet: Osei — 1965

Reference:        British lightweight sports-car class, first generation
Domain:           Vehicle model
Date:             production-pending
Target in game:   Osei, era 1 of 4 (`30` §3.3)

---

## 1. What is the response?
A tiny, low, open or barely-covered two-seater — the defining shape of
the entire lightweight-sports-car category. Minimal bodywork, exposed or
barely-shrouded wheel arches, and a small, high-revving four-cylinder
under a low, short bonnet.

## 2. Feature inventory
1. Very low overall height relative to any other car in the roster
2. Cycle-fender or minimally-shrouded wheel arches
3. Small round headlamps, often pod-mounted
4. Short, low bonnet line
5. Minimal chrome, functional trim only
6. Wire or basic steel wheels, small diameter
7. Fold-flat or removable soft-top (if roofed at all)
8. Rear-mounted spare wheel or minimal boot presence

| 6 | Small wire/steel wheels | No | Generic period wheel convention |

| 8 | Minimal boot | No | Functional consequence of the weight-removal philosophy |
