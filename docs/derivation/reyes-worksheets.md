---

## 1. What is the response?
A low, rear-engined coupe with a rounded, insect-eyed front end,
minimal overhangs, and a small rear deck concealing the engine. Early
homologation variants of this class add a modest ducktail spoiler and
flared rear arches over wider rubber — the first visible sign of the
aero philosophy that defines this rival for the next four decades.

## 2. Feature inventory

3. Long, sloped front overhang housing a small front trunk

5. Flared rear wheel arches

7. Five-spoke alloy wheel style, common across the whole class
8. Fastback roofline sloping to the rear deck
