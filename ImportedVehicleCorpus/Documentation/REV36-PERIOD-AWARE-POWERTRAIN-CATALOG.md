# Rev36 — Period-Aware Powertrain & Complete Vehicle Catalog

Rev36 makes the master powertrain catalog aware of career year. Parts cannot appear before their introduction year. Retired parts transition into used-original, NOS (for a limited post-production window), reproduction, salvage, and engineered-swap channels instead of disappearing.

## Powertrain families

15 engine families and 11 transmission/transaxle families are authored. The ZF truck manual and Pantera-style ZF transaxle are intentionally separate families because they are mechanically and historically distinct.

All supplied historical performance numbers, exact dimensions, capacities, clearances, torque specifications, application claims, and calibration maps remain research-gated unless independently verified.

## Compatibility

Catalog availability does not imply fitment. Compatibility evaluates architecture and can require mounts/crossmembers, packaging survey, later-era controls, fuel-system changes, cooling, exhaust, driveshaft/halfshaft changes, rear structure, shifter/control rerouting, and period-authenticity waivers. Hard geometry conflicts remain possible.

## Complete player-facing catalog

Rev36 exposes 186 authored player-facing vehicle entries across production heroes, heritage/reference platforms, Mustang heritage, fictional Vanguard specials, and the secret Apex Revenant. Heritage/reference entries are engineering/reference content and should not be confused with completed licensed production assets.
